Chapter 01 — The Landscape

SVH Serves a 19-County Region with Stark Disparities

Stormont Vail Health's service area spans from the Kansas City metro (Wyandotte, Douglas) to rural agricultural counties in the east and southeast — a region where socioeconomic conditions vary enormously.

Core vs. Periphery
Shawnee County (Topeka) accounts for 78% of all patient journeys. Nine counties outside Shawnee contribute significant volume but face far higher social vulnerability.
Wyandotte: The Highest-Risk County
SVI 0.755 — top of the vulnerability scale. 17.3% uninsured, 22.7% food insecure, 40.7% obesity rate. Structural barriers to care are extreme.
Pottawatomie: The Model County
SVI 0.286 — lowest vulnerability. 82% MyChart activation. 0.2% escalation rate. Shows what's possible with strong preventive engagement.
Chapter 02 — Vulnerability

Social Vulnerability Clusters in the East & Kansas City Corridor

The CDC Social Vulnerability Index aggregates poverty, disability, minority status, and housing instability into a single 0–1 score. High-SVI communities face compounding barriers that drive patients toward emergency care rather than preventive medicine.

Wyandotte (0.755) & Montgomery (0.737)
The two highest-SVI counties are geographically isolated from each other — suggesting system-wide structural challenges, not a single geographic cluster.
SVI Theme 3 (Minority/Language)
Geary County scores 0.816 on this theme — driven by a large military population at Fort Riley with diverse demographics and high transience, creating unique care continuity challenges.
Theme 4: Housing & Transport
Riley (0.740), Crawford (0.672), and Lyon (0.638) counties show high housing instability and transport vulnerability — predicting high missed-appointment rates.
Chapter 03 — Health Burden

Chronic Disease Rates Are Highest Where Access Is Worst

CDC PLACES model-based estimates reveal significant county-level variation in chronic disease prevalence. Counties with the highest rates are precisely those with the most structural barriers to preventive care — a self-reinforcing cycle.

Shawnee: 12.5% Diabetes, 39% High BP
Despite being the largest patient population with high MyChart engagement, Shawnee shows a serious chronic disease burden — suggesting a large at-risk population that SVH is actively managing.
Wyandotte: 14.5% Diabetes, 40.7% Obesity
The highest combined chronic disease burden in the service area, in the county with the least insurance coverage. These patients are most likely to arrive in crisis.
Osage: 41.8% Obesity
Highest obesity rate in SVH service area — a leading predictor of cardiovascular disease, diabetes, and joint disorders, all high-cost conditions for health systems.
Chapter 04 — Clinical Outcomes

Geary County Patients Escalate at 2× the System Average

Mapping SVH escalation rates — the proportion of journeys that include an ED visit or inpatient admission — reveals a striking geographic pattern: the counties with the most social vulnerability have the highest escalation rates.

Geary: 24.1% Escalation Rate
More than 1 in 4 patient journeys originating in Geary County escalate to emergency or inpatient care. The system average is 10.8% (Shawnee). This suggests a preventive care gap of significant scale.
Shawnee Dominates Volume
With 2.04M journeys (78% of total), Shawnee's 10.8% escalation rate represents ~220,000 escalation events — the largest absolute burden in the system.
Lyon & Riley: Near-Zero Escalation
These counties, despite 270K combined journeys, show escalation rates of 0.1–0.3% — likely reflecting the department mix and patient population SVH serves there (predominantly outpatient).

The Geography of Health Inequity
Is an Actionable Roadmap

SVH now has a data-driven map of where intervention will yield the greatest return. The spatial concentration of high SVI, chronic disease burden, and escalation rates points to four strategic priorities for the board.

🎯
Target Geary County
Deploy community health workers and telehealth pilots to address the 24.1% escalation rate among the 163K patient journeys originating there.
📱
Close the Digital Divide
Douglas and Wyandotte — both high-vulnerability — have the lowest MyChart engagement. Targeted digital navigator programs can unlock preventive outreach.
🍎
SDOH Screening at Scale
Wyandotte (22.7% food insecure) and Geary (19.5%) need food security and transportation screening embedded in every SVH encounter workflow.
📋
Export the Pottawatomie Model
0.2% escalation, 82% MyChart active, SVI 0.286 — analyze what's working in Pottawatomie and apply it systematically to Osage and Jefferson.
