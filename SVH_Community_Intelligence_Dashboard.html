Overall Vulnerability Profile — SVH Service Area CDC SVI 2022
⚠ High-Priority Counties
Wyandotte (0.755), Montgomery (0.737), and Geary (0.626) face the greatest combined socioeconomic burden. Geary is especially actionable: it sends significant patient volume to SVH but has the highest escalation rate in the system — suggesting unmet preventive care needs.
Health Burden Comparison — Top 5 Counties CDC PLACES 2024
📈 Geary & Shawnee Lead in Chronic Disease
Shawnee has the highest diabetes (12.5%) and high blood pressure (39%) rates. Osage has the highest obesity rate (41.8%). These chronic conditions directly drive ED escalation and longer patient journeys.
Key Findings for the Board
Finding 1: Geary County is an intervention priority.
24.1% escalation rate + SVI 0.626 + 13.1% uninsured = structural barriers to preventive care driving emergency utilization.
Finding 2: Digital engagement gap in Wyandotte & Douglas.
Despite high vulnerability, Wyandotte and Douglas have the lowest MyChart activation rates — cutting off preventive communication pathways.
Finding 3: Transport + food insecurity cluster.
Wyandotte, Geary, and Lyon show high co-occurrence of food insecurity (17–23%) and transportation barriers (9–11%) — classic SDOH risk factors for missed appointments and delayed care.
Opportunity: Pottawatomie is low-risk, high-engagement.
SVI 0.286, 82% MyChart active, 0.2% escalation — a model for what effective preventive care looks like. Lessons should be exported system-wide.

SVI vs. Escalation Rate SVH + CDC SVI
Key Signal: Higher Vulnerability = More Escalations
Geary County (SVI 0.626) has a 24.1% escalation rate — more than double Shawnee's 10.8% despite lower overall volume. Communities with higher vulnerability indices are more likely to use emergency care rather than preventive outpatient services.
MyChart Engagement vs. Vulnerability SVH + CDC SVI
Digital Divide Warning
Douglas County (lowest escalation but only 48.4% MyChart activation) and Wyandotte (highest SVI, likely low activation) suggest a digital engagement gap in the most vulnerable communities — undermining preventive outreach.
