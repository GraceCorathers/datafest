Bridging the Gap:
The Geography of Patient Crises

An executive analysis responding directly to the DataFest 2026 Challenge. By mapping 2.63 million Stormont Vail Health clinical encounters, we address the core research questions: identifying systemic gaps in longitudinal care, measuring the impact of social vulnerability on patient outcomes, and evaluating how geographic and demographic factors isolate patients from continuous outpatient management.

Follow The Journey ↓

Meet Samantha.

She is fighting to stay healthy.

Samantha is a real representation of the data. Like 64.6% of patients surveyed across our healthcare system, she is currently battling severe socio-economic stress on top of her physical health conditions.

But her challenge isn't a medical mystery. It's a structural failure in how the health system reaches out to our most vulnerable neighbors.

Caught in the Loop.

Crisis-Driven Care

A statistical archetype identifying patients whose journey entirely lacks continuous outpatient management. Rather than routine check-ups, their only healthcare interaction is severe, repeated Emergency Department visits.

Samantha hasn't seen a primary care provider in over 90 days. But she's not alone. Our data reveals a staggering 46,674 SVH patients trapped in the exact same "Crisis-Driven" loop.

We wait until her condition is severe enough for an ER visit, stabilize her, and release her back into the very environment that triggered the crisis.

It's Bigger Than The Hospital.

SDOH (Social Determinants of Health)

The non-medical factors—such as housing insecurity, lack of transportation, and neighborhood vulnerability—that influence 80% of clinical health outcomes.

If we want to fix Samantha's care journey, we cannot just look at hospital waiting times. We must look at the community level. We have to intervene where she lives, providing mobile care, transportation networks, and active neighborhood engagement.

To understand exactly where we must deploy these community resources, we have plotted the 3-year clinical dataset across the geography of Kansas.
