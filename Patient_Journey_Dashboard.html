What is a Patient Journey?

A journey is defined as all encounters for a single patient linked to the same ICD-10 diagnosis code (DiagnosisValue). It begins at first diagnosis and tracks every follow-up, lab, imaging, and hospital visit tied to that condition.

Journey ID = PatientID + DiagnosisValue. Using DiagnosisValue (not PrimaryDiagnosisKey) ensures stability across federal coding updates.

Key caveat: 60.7% of journeys consist of a single visit. These may be resolved conditions, or patients who did not return — a clinically meaningful distinction we cannot distinguish without outcome data.
Truncation: 99,220 journeys (3.8%) are left/right-truncated — they started before Jan 2022 or were still active in Dec 2025. Journey duration metrics are conservative estimates for these.

Escalation Analysis (ED + Inpatient)

Abdominal/pelvic pain (R10) has a 22.3% escalation rate — the highest among high-volume diagnoses. These patients frequently convert to ED visits, suggesting potential for earlier intervention.
Geary County has the highest escalation rate at 24.1% — more than double the state average (10.8% in Shawnee). Junction City is the major population center in Geary County, suggesting a geographic disparity worth investigating.

Demographics & Care Patterns

Age insight: Older patients (65–79) have the most encounters per journey (2.61 avg) and longest journey durations (180d avg) — consistent with chronic disease management. But their escalation rate is relatively low (6.4%), suggesting better-managed ongoing care.
Youth escalation: Patients under 18 have a 14.6% escalation rate and patients 18–34 have 14.4% — both well above the 65–79 rate (6.4%). Young patients hitting EDs at high rates warrants investigation.

Trends Over Time

Apparent decline in avg_length for recent quarters: This is a right-truncation artifact. Journeys starting in 2025 haven't had time to accumulate follow-up visits yet. Journey counts remain stable (160–167K per quarter in 2024–2025).
Escalation rate stabilized at ~10% from 2023 onwards, up from 8.1% in 2022. The 2022 figure may reflect incomplete data capture for that year (admissions coverage).

Geographic Distribution

Geary County escalation rate = 24.1% — more than double Shawnee County (10.8%). This is the most striking geographic disparity in the data. Geary County includes Junction City and Fort Riley military base.
Shawnee County dominates: 77.5% of journeys originate there (Topeka HQ). Meaningful rural analysis is possible for the remaining counties.
